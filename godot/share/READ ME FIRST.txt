ATC RAYCRASH (fan recreation, for private play)
================================================

This is a private, non-commercial recreation of ATC Raycrash (CodiNET, 2005), made for
playing among friends. It contains the original game's art and sound. Please don't share
it further or post it anywhere.

STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (for example your Desktop). Don't run it from inside
   the zip.
2. Double-click "ATC Raycrash.exe".
3. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC", because the game isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". This only happens the first time.
4. If the game prints errors or won't start, run "ATC Raycrash.console.exe" instead: it
   opens the same game with a text window showing what went wrong. Send a screenshot of it.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and graphics that support OpenGL 3.3, which is almost
any laptop from the last ten years. A mouse is strongly recommended.

FIRST STEPS
-----------
- Login: type any name as your ID (the password is ignored), then ACCESS.
- Character select: the arrows pick your mech, ENTER goes on.
- Main menu:
    MATCH      play with others (room list and game rooms)
    TUTORIAL   practice against a target dummy
    ITEM SHOP  choose your weapons (nothing costs anything)
    OPTION     volume, full screen, tooltips

CONTROLS
--------
    W A S D          move
    Mouse            aim
    Left click       attack (hold for some weapons)
    Right click      boost (hold)
    1 2 3 4          weapon slots
    Space            raise or lower your guard
    C                grab: dash ahead and catch whoever you meet
    X                jump: a one-second hop most attacks pass under (every 5 s)
    Shift            shove: knock everyone close by away (every 20 s)
    F                next lock-on target (missiles)
    Tab              everyone's health
    + / -            camera zoom
    M                sound on or off
    Esc              leave the battle

Sword combos: tap a movement key toward the way you are facing, then click.

While holding someone after a grab, you have 1.5 s to enter a grab combo:
    a direction key, then left click            throw them that way
    left, right, then right click               spark (70 damage)
    right, right, left, left, then right click  drain (45 damage, you heal 30)
Each mech also has its own special attack, only when you are at 10% health or less:
    Zeid      right right left right left down right, then right click
    Red Kite  right left right left down right right, then right click
    Gigas     left down right left right left down right, then right click
    Deff      right left down right left down right, then right click
    Alicia    left down right left down right left, then right click
Directions are the screen's (W A S D or the arrow keys), not your mech's facing.

PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
On the same Wi-Fi or home network:
  One player goes to MATCH, CREATE, and makes a room. The others go to MATCH and the room
  appears in the list: click it twice to go in. The room's creator presses START.

From different places (over the internet), use Tailscale (free, tailscale.com):
  1. Both players install Tailscale and sign in. The host invites the other player to their
     tailnet, or shares their computer with them from Tailscale's admin page.
  2. The host creates a room. The room's chat shows the host's addresses; the Tailscale one
     starts with 100.
  3. The other player types that address into the room list's room-name box at the top,
     then clicks GO.
  4. To check the connection, run "tailscale ping 100.x.y.z" (the host's address). "via" an
     address means a direct connection; "via DERP" means it goes through Tailscale's relay
     servers, which is slower.
  The player with the fastest upload speed should host, on a computer rather than a phone.

The first time you host, Windows may ask whether to allow the game through the firewall.
Allow it on private networks, or nobody can join you.
